# Proposal: a UCompute-branded GitHub organization as the public home

**Observation.** The public repository lives under the founder's personal handle, and the website's deploy integration is installed on that account. Every public link therefore exposes the personal identity the charter asks Growth to protect. On 2026-10-30 the GitHub login `ucompute` was unregistered.

**Proposal.** The owner creates the free organization `ucompute` now (reserving the name) and, when source publication is decided (engineering `docs/release/github-sync-proposal-2026-10-29.md` §4), the redacted public snapshot is published under `ucompute/ucompute` rather than under the personal account; the current public repository is archived with a pointer. Engineering decides the repository mechanics; Growth only asks that the public home carry the project's identity.

**Also.** A project mailbox (`hello@ucompute.org` through Cloudflare Email Routing) becomes the contact for the security policy, community posts and platform accounts, so no personal address appears publicly.

**Cost.** Free. Effort: minutes. Reversible.
