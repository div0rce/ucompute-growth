# ucompute-growth (private)

The operating repository of **UCompute Growth**, the autonomous marketing, developer-relations and distribution workstream for UCompute. Every session, local or cloud, starts from `CLAUDE.md` and `runbook/RUNBOOK.md` and ends with a pushed commit.

For the owner, three files matter:
- `journal/STATE.md`: where things stand and what happens next.
- `journal/OWNER-ASKS.md`: the short list of one-time actions only you can do. Nothing else waits on you.
- `channels/REGISTRY.md`: where Growth may publish. Nothing is published until a row says AUTHORIZED with your words quoted.

Cloud routines run daily (13:00 UTC) and weekly (Mondays 14:00 UTC): https://claude.ai/code/routines. Their ids are in `journal/STATE.md`.
